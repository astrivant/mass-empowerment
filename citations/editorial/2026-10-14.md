# Editorial revision — 14 October 2026

This revision changes the fiction and illustrations independently of the weekly
technical-source review. Earlier versions remain in Git history, including the
[preceding reader edition](https://github.com/astrivant/mass-empowerment/tree/f3cabe1e93142792013b280aedf7eff945e4add8).

## Story

The introduction establishes Lowreach in the fictional year 2189 and clarifies the
roles of service operators, municipal authorities, independent utilities and local
communities. Ongoing laws and conditions use present tense; founding history remains
past tense. Named organizations and events receive consistent capitalization.

Additional passages ground the competition and cooperation among people, plants
and embodied AI in electricity, light, food, shelter and maintenance. Mara's existing
repair and programming skills lead to a dangerous assignment for Undertow's
Blindroom division. The consequences continue through the negotiations, storm and
aftermath; the ending offers limited freedom with continuing costs and obligations.
Undertow and Blindroom are fictional organizations.

## Artwork and evidence

The approved title image and six revised scene illustrations are preserved as
separate lossless PNGs and referenced by the editable source. The scene revisions
retain their original placements and add sparse flying AI, supported climbing
machines and planting appropriate to each setting. The illustration inventory
records the exact images and their provenance.

The eight technical footnotes, their immutable public-code citations and the
nonfiction evidence appendix remain unchanged. This editorial revision makes no
new claim that the fictional capabilities are implemented by those sources.
The current chapter and prose-only checksums establish the baseline for future
citation-only reviews.

## Reading PDF

The published PDF is an optimized reading copy of the reviewed 102-page master.
Illustrations use JPEG quality 95 with no chroma subsampling and retain their pixel
dimensions. All text, links, bookmarks and page dimensions are unchanged. The seven
separate source PNGs remain lossless. The full-quality master is preserved separately;
its checksum and the reading copy checksum are recorded in `EDITION.json`.
