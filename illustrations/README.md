# Illustration sources

These seven separate PNGs contain the artwork used in *The Hours We Keep*. The cover is 1536 × 1024 pixels; the six interior plates are 1024 × 1536. All are 8-bit RGB, with no alpha channel.

## Revised cover

The current cover is a new AI-assisted revision made on 14 October 2026. Endless constructed depth, inhabited scaffolds, epiphytic plants, AI taxis and varied crawling machines, with localized neon and geometric holograms against a bleak industrial environment. The generated PNG is retained without raster postprocessing or recompression. It is not a pixel recovery of the earlier cover. The previous cover remains in Git history and is linked in the inventory.

## Interior illustrations

The six interiors were recovered from the archived high-resolution review master, whose image streams use lossless compression. No interior image was resized, recolored, regenerated or edited. Every recovered PNG’s decoded RGB pixels were checked against the corresponding master image stream and match exactly.

The original generated interior PNG file containers remain unavailable. These are lossless pixel recoveries, not byte-for-byte copies of those original files, and they were not extracted from the smaller JPEG-compressed reading PDF. The inventory records both the recovered PNG hashes and the previously recorded original hashes.

`provenance.json` records dimensions, checksums, alt text and chapter placement. The cover belongs in the front matter; interior plates follow chapters 1, 3, 6, 9, 11 and 14. Four plates depict story scenes and three provide wider world context.
