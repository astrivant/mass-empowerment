# Illustration sources

Seven lossless RGB PNGs supply the cover and six full-page interior illustrations in The Hours We Keep. The cover is 1536 × 1024 pixels; the interiors are 1024 × 1536 pixels, placed after chapters 1, 3, 6, 9, 11 and 14.

This revision adds a varied epiphyte mix within existing planted areas while retaining each composition and its characters, machinery, lighting and narrative role. Ceratostema-, Oncidium- and Grammatophyllum-inspired forms vary by scene. A compact Acianthera recurva-inspired miniature orchid appears in the workshop illustration. These are stylized scene-scale plant forms, not taxonomically diagnostic botanical plates.

The generated PNG containers are preserved without raster postprocessing. The master PDF embeds their RGB pixels losslessly. The smaller reading PDF uses JPEG quality 95 at the same pixel dimensions; its text, vector ornaments, map and links are unchanged. The PNGs remain the lossless illustration sources.

provenance.json records file dimensions, hashes, alt text, scene placement, botanical references and previous-revision provenance. Previous published artwork remains in repository history.
