# Illustration sources

These seven separate PNGs contain the artwork used in *The Hours We Keep*. The approved cover is 1536 × 1024 pixels; the six revised interior plates are 1024 × 1536. All are 8-bit RGB, with no alpha channel.

The cover and six interiors are AI-assisted revisions of the previously recovered artwork, made on 14 October 2026. Each interior preserves its scene placement while adding restrained planting, practical machine interfaces and supported maintenance machinery appropriate to its setting. Controlled water at the intake and visible rain at the switchhouse remain distinct.

The generated PNGs are preserved without raster postprocessing or recompression. These are new illustrations, not pixel-identical recoveries of the previous edition. Previous artwork and its provenance remain in Git history and are linked in the inventory.

`provenance.json` records dimensions, checksums, alt text, chapter placement and changes. The cover belongs in the front matter; interior plates follow chapters 1, 3, 6, 9, 11 and 14. Four plates depict story scenes and three provide wider world context.
