# Border and divider sources

The book retains its recovered circuit, network and geometric edge motifs and electronic separator. The pencil separator is now an original cursive-style vector flourish with asymmetric loops and no lettering. Its SVG, vector PDF and transparent 600-dpi PNG share a 129 × 16 point canvas.

The botanical subfolder contains three horizontal Corinthian dividers and six natural hanging-plant margins, each supplied as SVG, vector PDF and transparent 600-dpi PNG. Botanical dividers rotate through the existing nature slots; occasional paired hanging plants descend from both top corners at varied proportional lengths, replacing all geometric edge motifs and corner brackets on those pages. Electronic and cursive separators retain their alternating roles.

The recovered original orchid separator remains available as a historical source asset. The current book uses the three botanical horizontal variants instead. The original pencil provenance is preserved within its replacement entry in manifest.json. Artwork is either recovered from the earlier book or newly drawn, as its individual provenance states.
