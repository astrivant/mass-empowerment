## A future we can still shape

*The Hours We Keep* is an invitation to curate a vision together. Its appendix compares ten explicit indicators across two independent columns: high-level RFC-theme coverage and evidence already observable in public life. On the 13 October 2026 review, seven indicators have explicit conceptual coverage; two meet their real-world occurrence criteria, six have partial evidence and two remain unknown.

These are shares of a selected checklist, not percentages of the future, implementation progress or predictions. A data center beside homes is one documented resemblance; it does not make the story's political order inevitable. Proposals, working components and social outcomes remain distinct.

We can debate whether emerging capabilities concentrate decision-making in a technocracy, support a more just and identity-safe world, or lead to other arrangements. The practical questions are who can consent, refuse, earn, challenge and leave. No technology settles those questions by itself.

Read the [appendix](vision/APPENDIX.md), inspect the [indicator dataset](vision/vision-indicators.csv) and [public source register](vision/public-sources.json), and propose corrections with dated public evidence. Include counterexamples and affected communities' perspectives. The conceptual mapping is an editorial summary; its confidential source corpus is not part of this publication. Do not post private research, personal data or company-specific plans. The [method](vision/METHODOLOGY.md) explains how to revise the denominator transparently rather than making the chart look more certain.

## Evidence and revisions

The story's eight footnotes identify technical inspirations and their limits. Each GitHub source is pinned to the reviewed commit. The appendix uses a separate set of public primary sources. Historical releases establish documented occurrences within their scope; they do not establish present universal availability, reliable autonomy or current law. No source establishes transfer of subjective human identity.

Future citation edits should preserve the literary text unless a separate editorial change is intended. Past editions and the reasons for changes should remain recoverable. The indicator definitions and denominator must be versioned when the evidence map changes.

## Contributing

Offer a precise correction, counterexample or proposed indicator, with a public primary source, its publication date, geographic and operational scope, and relevant limits. Separate a standard from its implementation, an announcement from an outcome and a technical capability from a social benefit.

Do not submit confidential proposals, personal records, credentials or private company details. Public contributors can inspect and challenge the real-world evidence assessment; the confidential conceptual mapping cannot be independently reproduced from this bundle alone.

## Typography and artwork

Story text is embedded EB Garamond at 11 points with 16.5-point leading, left aligned. Other type sizes are balanced around it. The PDF retains linked Contents, bookmarks and genuine page footnotes. Its reading copy compresses illustrations without reducing their original pixel dimensions; text and the evidence matrix remain vector content.

The cover and six interior plates are original AI-assisted illustrations embedded in the PDF and linked from the Markdown. [Separate PNGs and provenance](illustrations/README.md) include the revised AI-generated cover and six full-resolution interior pixel recoveries. The cover adds inhabited scaffolds, plants, AI taxis, crawling machines and localized neon holograms within endless industrial structures; the six interiors preserve their archived master pixels exactly. The original interior PNG containers remain unavailable. The story-scene/world-context allocation is four/three, approximately 57/43.

Headings and ornaments use a muted gray-brown derivative of the public [Tiger Lily Plants](https://tiger-lily-plants.com/) theme. This is a design reference, not an affiliation claim.

The PDF embeds EB Garamond and DejaVu for reading. No new general license is assigned here to the original prose or illustrations.

[Page-border icons and section dividers](ornaments/README.md) are available as eleven transparent 600-DPI PNGs, with a complete placement inventory. They are rasterizations of the original vector artwork.
